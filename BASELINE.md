## 1. Quantitative Philosophy: No "Magic Numbers" Before Data Runs

In accordance with **Dr. Marcos López de Prado**, **Dr. Ernest P. Chan**, and **Euan Sinclair**:
- A baseline is an **empirical measurement of ground truth**, NOT a wish list.
- We strictly separate **Structural Priors (the un-optimized entry/exit rules)** from **Observed Results (the empirical metrics)**.
- **The Anti-Snooping Rule:** The baseline rules are fixed *beforehand* as un-optimized heuristics. We are strictly forbidden from tweaking parameters (e.g., changing 20 days to 18 days, or changing 1.5x delivery to 1.8x) after viewing backtest results.
- **No Pre-Assumed Performance:** The performance table contains zero fabricated returns. All baseline values are populated directly from the output of the Milestone 1 backtest on cleaned Bhavcopy data.

## 2. The Zero-ML Baseline Specification (Fixed Structural Priors)

### 2.1 Baseline Entry Conditions (Un-Optimized Prior)
Evaluated strictly at Market Close (15:30 IST) of Day $T$:
1. **20-Day Donchian Breakout:** Stock closes above its 20-day high (standard 1-month institutional cycle):
   $$Close_T > \max(High_{T-20} \dots High_{T-1})$$
2. **Institutional Accumulation Volume:** Cash delivery volume expands above its monthly baseline:
   $$\text{Delivery Volume}_T > 1.5 \times SMA_{20}(\text{Delivery Volume})_T$$
3. **F&O Long Buildup:** Stock Futures Open Interest expands alongside price:
   $$\Delta OI_T > +5.0\% \quad \text{and} \quad Close_T > Close_{T-1}$$

### 2.2 Baseline Execution & Triple Barrier Rule
- **Entry Execution:** Day $T+1$ Market Open (09:15–09:20 IST) with $0.10\%$ slippage and full statutory costs.
- **Upper Horizontal Barrier ($R^+$):** $P_0 + 2.0 \times \text{Daily } ATR_{14}$ ($y = +1$)
- **Lower Horizontal Barrier ($R^-$):** $P_0 - 1.0 \times \text{Daily } ATR_{14}$ ($y = -1$)
- **Vertical Time Barrier ($T$):** Exit at Close of Day $T+3$ if neither horizontal barrier is touched ($y = 0$).
- **Gap-Over Stop Handling:** If Day $T+2$ opens below the stop price, the fill occurs at the actual gap Open price (recording the real-world gap loss).

## 4. The Scientific "Win Rule" for Experiments

An experiment (e.g., LightGBM Meta-Labeling, Sector Momentum, Dynamic Volatility Filter) is evaluated **strictly relative to the Baseline's actual empirical performance**:

$$\mathbf{\text{WIN}} \iff \mathbf{\Delta \text{Calmar Ratio} \ge +20\%} \quad \text{AND} \quad \mathbf{\text{Max Drawdown} \le \text{Baseline Actual Max Drawdown}}$$

### 4.1 Why the Calmar & Drawdown Balance is the Ultimate Standard
- **Risk-Reduction Wins are Celebrated:** An experiment that lowers CAGR by $1\%$ but cuts Max Drawdown from $12\%$ to $6\%$ is a **massive institutional win** (Calmar jumps $\approx +80\%$). On a ₹5L account, preventing capital drawdown preserves psychological stamina and keeps compounding alive.
- **Trade Filtration Wins:** An experiment (such as Meta-Labeling) that rejects 35% of false breakouts, improves Net Expectancy per trade, and reduces Drawdown is a **decisive win**, even if total nominal return stays flat.

### 4.2 Instant Disqualification Criteria (Fatal Failures)
An experiment is immediately **REJECTED**, regardless of nominal profit, if:
1. **Drawdown Blowout:** Maximum Drawdown exceeds **$12.0\%$** of account equity (₹60,000 on ₹5L).
2. **Negative Skewness / Tail Risk:** Positive frequency rises, but average loss grows to $> 2.5\times$ average win (picking pennies in front of a steamroller).
3. **Frictional Degradation:** Net Expectancy per trade drops below **$+0.15 \text{ ATR}$** (churning too much capital and paying excessive exchange fees).
4. **DSR Failure:** Deflated Sharpe Ratio drops below $0.90$ (indicating performance was curve-fitted through trial snooping).
5. **Single-Year Concentration:** More than $70\%$ of cumulative profits were generated in a single abnormal year (e.g., 2020) while remaining stagnant the rest of the time.
